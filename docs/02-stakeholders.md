Stakeholder Identification

1. Introduction

Stakeholders are individuals or groups who affect, are affected by, or have an interest in the Spare Parts Management System. Identifying stakeholders helps determine their needs and expectations.

2. Stakeholders Table

ID	Stakeholder	Role	Main Needs and Expectations
ST-01	Customer	Searches for products and places orders	Easy product search, accurate product information, simple ordering, and order tracking
ST-02	Store Manager	Manages products and customer orders	Ability to add, update, and deactivate products and manage order statuses
ST-03	Warehouse Staff	Monitors product inventory	Accurate inventory information and the ability to identify low-stock products
ST-04	Sales Support Staff	Assists customers with questions and orders	Access to order information and the ability to help customers track their orders
ST-05	Business Owner	Defines business goals and monitors operations	Improved sales management, accurate information, and more efficient order processing
ST-06	Software Development Team	Designs, develops, and maintains the system	Clear requirements, defined system scope, and maintainable software

3. Conclusion

The main stakeholders of the system are customers and the store manager because they directly interact with its primary features. Warehouse staff, sales support staff, the business owner, and the software development team may also have an interest in the system and influence its requirements.

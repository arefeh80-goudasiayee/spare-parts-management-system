Stakeholder Identification

1. Introduction

Stakeholders are individuals or groups who affect, are affected by, or have an interest in the Spare Parts Management System.

2. Stakeholders Table

| ID | Stakeholder | Role | Main Needs and Expectations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ST-01 | Customer | Searches for products and places orders | Easy product search, accurate product information, simple ordering, and order tracking |
| ST-02 | Store Manager | Manages products and customer orders | Ability to add, update, and deactivate products and manage order statuses |
| ST-03 | Warehouse Staff | Monitors product inventory | Accurate inventory information and low-stock alerts |
| ST-04 | Sales Support Staff | Assists customers | Access to order information and customer support tools |
| ST-05 | Business Owner | Defines business goals | Improved sales management and efficient order processing |
| ST-06 | Software Development Team | Develops and maintains the system | Clear requirements and maintainable software |

3. Conclusion

Customers and the store manager are the primary stakeholders because they directly interact with the main system features. Other stakeholders may influence the system requirements or benefit from its operation.
